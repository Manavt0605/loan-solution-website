ABOUT THE LOAN

What is a Personal Loan?

Life comes with unexpected expenses and important personal goals. A Personal Loan can provide financial support for eligible personal requirements.

A Personal Loan is commonly an unsecured loan offered based on factors such as income, credit history, and repayment capacity. It may be used for a range of eligible personal expenses.
