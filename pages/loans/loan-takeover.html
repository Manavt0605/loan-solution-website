ABOUT THE LOAN

What is a Loan Takeover?

Are you currently paying an existing loan and looking for a different lender or improved repayment structure? A Loan Takeover may help eligible borrowers transfer their outstanding loan balance.

A Loan Takeover, also known as a Balance Transfer, allows an eligible borrower to transfer an existing loan from one lender to another, subject to approval and lender terms.

Why Consider a Loan Takeover?

You may consider a loan takeover to explore:

  • ✔ Competitive interest rate options
  • ✔ Improved EMI structure
  • ✔ Better repayment tenure options
  • ✔ Additional funding opportunities, where eligible
  • ✔ Improved service and loan features
PROCESS

How Does a Loan Takeover Work?

01

Step 1: Review Your Existing Loan

Check your outstanding balance, EMI, interest rate, and remaining tenure.

02

Step 2: Check Eligibility

The new lender evaluates your income, credit profile, repayment history, and existing loan details.

03

Step 3: Submit Documents

Provide the required documents and existing loan information.

04

Step 4: Loan Approval

If approved, the new lender may arrange the transfer of the outstanding loan amount according to applicable procedures.

05

Step 5: Continue Repayment

You begin repayment according to the new loan agreement.
