ABOUT THE LOAN

What is a Mortgage Loan?

Do you own residential or commercial property? A Mortgage Loan can help you access funds by using your property as security.

A Mortgage Loan, also known as a Loan Against Property in many lending contexts, is a secured loan where an eligible property is pledged as collateral. The funds may be used for eligible personal or business requirements, subject to lender terms and conditions.
