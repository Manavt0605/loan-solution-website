ABOUT THE LOAN

What is a Project Loan?

Large projects require careful planning and proper financial support. A Project Loan can help eligible businesses and developers arrange funding for planned projects.

A Project Loan is financing designed for eligible projects where funds may be required for construction, development, infrastructure, expansion, or other approved project activities.
