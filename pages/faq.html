FAQ

Common Questions

What types of loans does Loan Solution help with?

We provide guidance for various loan requirements including home loans, mortgage loans, project loans, business loans, personal loans, education loans, vehicle loans and loan takeover options.

How do I apply for a loan?

You can submit an enquiry through our Apply Now page. Our team can then discuss your requirement and explain the next steps.

What documents are generally required?

Documents can vary depending on the lender, loan type and applicant profile. Commonly requested documents may include identity proof, address proof, income documents, bank statements and property documents where applicable.

Can self-employed people apply for loans?

Self-employed applicants may be considered, subject to the eligibility criteria and assessment requirements of the relevant lender.

What is an EMI?

EMI stands for Equated Monthly Instalment. It is the amount paid periodically towards repayment of a loan, including applicable principal and interest.

Does Loan Solution guarantee loan approval?

No. Loan approval is subject to the eligibility criteria, assessment and policies of the relevant lender.

Can I transfer my existing loan?

Depending on your situation, you may explore balance transfer or loan takeover options. Eligibility and terms depend on the lender.

How can I contact Loan Solution?

You can contact us through the Contact Us page or submit an enquiry using the Apply Now form.
